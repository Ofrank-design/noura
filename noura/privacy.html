Privacy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We collect the information you give us: your contact details, your answers to the nutrition assessment, your food log, check ins and messages, and any documents you choose to share. We also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, to keep the site working.

How we use it

We use your information to provide and improve your care, to schedule and confirm appointments, to send reminders you have asked for, and to keep the practice secure. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your information is seen by the practitioners and care staff who work with you. With your permission, we may share it with your physician or another clinician involved in your care. Service providers who help us run the practice, such as scheduling and payment providers, receive only what they need.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your information at any time by writing to hello@noura.example. You can change your email preferences from any message we send, and you can withdraw consent for optional uses whenever you like.

How we protect it

We limit access to the people who need it, use encrypted connections, and keep a record of who has accessed client information. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if something affects you.

Health information

Information about your health is especially sensitive. We keep it separate from marketing and use it only to deliver your care and to meet our professional and legal obligations.

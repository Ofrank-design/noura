NOURA IntelligenceDemonstration

General guidance, not medical advice. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

What to ask

Start with an ingredient, a trip or a menu.

The more specific the question, the more useful the answer. These are the kinds of things clients ask most.

  • What can I use instead of this ingredient?
  • I only have fifteen minutes. What can I make?
  • How do I eat well on a four day trip?
  • I am going out for dinner. How should I plan?
  • I missed lunch. What should I do now?
How it works

Useful, careful and honest about its limits.

It knows your plan

Inside the client portal, answers take your meal plan, preferences, allergies and schedule into account.

It asks a person when it should

Questions about medication, medical conditions or anything that changes your clinical plan go to your dietitian.

It never acts alone

Adjustments and swaps are sent for review. Your current plan stays in place until your dietitian approves a change.

Get support that knows you.

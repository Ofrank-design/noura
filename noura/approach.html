An approach built around listening.

Good nutrition care starts with understanding how you live. Everything we do, from the first conversation to the last review, is shaped by that.

01

Understand

Two people can eat the same meal for different reasons, with different results. We learn your history, routines, preferences and goals before we suggest anything.

02

Personalize

We build around your working week, your kitchen, your budget and your tastes, and we change the plan when your circumstances change.

03

Sustain

We favor small, repeatable improvements over dramatic overhauls, because the habits you can keep are the ones that work.

Evidence based, without the obsession

Rigorous about the science. Relaxed about the rules.

We follow current research and professional guidelines, explain our reasoning in plain language, and say so when the evidence is limited.

What we focus on

  • Regular, satisfying meals
  • Protein, fiber and variety
  • Energy, sleep and appetite cues
  • Planning that suits your week
  • Confidence when eating out and travelling

What we leave out

  • Forbidden foods and rigid rules
  • Guilt, shame and scare tactics
  • Unproven supplements and quick fixes
  • Calorie counting, unless it is useful to you
  • Advice that ignores your medical context
How care works

From first question to lasting habits.

Nutrition assessment

A short online questionnaire about your habits, schedule and goals. It produces a preliminary profile you can bring to your first session.

Consultation

A relaxed conversation with your dietitian, in our studio or by secure video, to agree on priorities.

Personalized plan

A meal plan, recipes and practical guidance that fit your routine, delivered in your client portal.

Client portal

Your plan, shopping lists, food log and messages in one place, available on your phone at any hour.

Ongoing support

Weekly check ins, regular follow ups and adjustments as your life changes.

Progress review

A clear look at what has improved, what to keep and what comes next.

A kitchen that works for you

A well stocked pantry does half the work.

Most good weeknight meals come from a handful of reliable staples. We help you build a pantry that makes the healthy choice the easy one, without a shopping list that takes over your weekend.

  • Olive oil, vinegar, spices and good salt
  • Canned beans, lentils, tomatoes and fish
  • Whole grains, oats and wholewheat pasta
  • Frozen vegetables and berries
  • Eggs, yogurt, nuts and seeds
Technology with a person behind it

NOURA Intelligence supports your dietitian.

It helps with practical questions between sessions and gives your dietitian a clearer picture of how your week is going. It does not diagnose, and it never changes your clinical plan on its own.

  • Clinical suggestions are reviewed by a registered dietitian
  • You can always message a person directly
  • Your information is used only to provide your care

Telehealth ready

Coordinated care

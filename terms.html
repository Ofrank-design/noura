Terms

The ground rules for using this website and our services.

Educational information

Articles, recipes and tools on this website are educational and do not replace individual medical advice. Please speak with your physician or a registered dietitian before making changes that affect your health.

Appointments and cancellations

You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Later changes or missed appointments may be charged in full. If something unavoidable happens, please contact us and we will do our best to help.

Payments

Fees are shown before you confirm a booking. Program fees can be paid in installments where offered. Receipts are provided after every payment, and refunds are considered case by case.

NOURA Intelligence

NOURA Intelligence provides general guidance and organizes information. It does not diagnose conditions or replace your dietitian. Clinical suggestions are reviewed by a registered dietitian before they become part of your plan.

Your responsibilities

Please give accurate information about your health, allergies and medication, and tell us promptly if anything changes. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Changes

We may update these terms from time to time. We will post the new version on this page, and significant changes will be communicated to clients directly.
